Data Engineer, specialising in GIS

I'm Asif, a UK-based Data Engineer. I cover the full data engineering toolkit — ingestion, ETL/ELT, data modelling, testing and orchestration — and specialise in GIS, adding the spatial element that most pipelines don't handle well.

At work I build and automate spatial workflows with FME Workbench and FME Flow, pull from ESRI REST services and public APIs, and land clean, well-modelled data in PostgreSQL/PostGIS. I hold the Cambridge Spark Level 5 Data Engineer qualification, passed with Distinction.

My industry background is in waste management, and I'm comfortable with property and real-estate data. Outside work I build end-to-end portfolio pipelines — most recently KerbRadar, which turns UK Traffic Regulation Order data into answers about where you can park.

I'm working towards senior data engineer and solution architect roles, which is why I'm going deep on Databricks, Spark internals, Delta Lake and streaming.
